Routines ​

Routines are recurring protected-time patterns — separate from one-off tasks and fixed events.

Routines vs tasks vs events ​

KindUse for
TasksActionable work with due dates, duration, priorities, dependencies, splittable scheduling
EventsTime blocks that reserve calendar time and act as busy constraints for the scheduler
RoutinesRepeated structures: morning planning, workouts, deep-work windows, weekly reviews

When to use routines ​

Use routines when the time pattern repeats (daily, weekly, monthly style cadence) and you want protected structure on the timeline without treating each occurrence as a separate task to complete.

Use tasks when you need due dates, completion, splitting around meetings, and dependency chains.

Creating routines ​

Open Routines from the sidebar, add a routine with title, cadence, and time window. Routines integrate with your calendar display according to the same recurrence rules as other repeating items.

For one-off meetings or appointments that should block scheduling, prefer events (from Calendar or task/event editor) or Google Calendar imports.
