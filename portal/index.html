RePackr

(2024)

An intuitive, accessible packing planner tool

Kaironaut

(2026) Featured

Navigate your timestream

Built to be the mother of all planner apps, with bespoke scheduling automation, Google Calendar integration, and research-backed personal productivity insights.

Kaironaut represents a project that has been on my heart for years, and is a chimera of some time management features I've seen across various productivity apps as well as others I've dreamed of but hadn't seen implemented before. It is the new and improved successor to another automated planner app I built, TemPad (2026), which in turn was the spiritual successor to AIsenhower (2023), a Julia desktop frontend for task management. See the Kaironaut docs for guides/setup (or just jump right in and try it out!).
